Box Method — Positions vs Options

Art of Counting · Unit 4

The Box Method
Counting — Positions vs Options
The Golden Rule
Boxes = POSITIONS (slots to fill)
NOT the number of available options
❌ 4 options available → draw 4 boxes    WRONG
✅ 2-digit number → draw 2 boxes        RIGHT
What we will work through today:

Using digits {2, 4, 6, 8} — how many 2-digit numbers can you make?

We will look at two cases: with repeats and without repeats, then you get two practice problems.
