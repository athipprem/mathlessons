Quick Review — Factor Tree

How to build a factor tree

1
Split the number into any two factors (not 1 and itself).
2
Keep splitting each branch until every end is a prime.
3
Collect all the primes at the ends — that's your answer.

Example: 36 — this was Q7 in Week 5

36 / \ 4 9 / \ / \ 2 2 3 3 36 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 3 = 2² × 3²
All four ends are prime ✓  →  36 = 2² × 3²

LCM via Prime Factorisation

This was Q8 in Week 5 — let's nail it

Find LCM(12, 18) using prime factorisation.

1
Factorise both numbers.
12 = 2² × 3 18 = 2 × 3²
2
For each prime, take the highest power from either number.
Highest power of 2 → 2² = 4 (from 12) Highest power of 3 → 3² = 9 (from 18)
3
Multiply the highest powers together.
LCM = 4 × 9 = 36
Check: 36 ÷ 12 = 3 ✓    36 ÷ 18 = 2 ✓  →  LCM(12, 18) = 36
