When two lines cross, something special happens 🔀

Two straight lines cross and make 4 angles. Look at the diagram below carefully.

Vertically opposite angles are ALWAYS equal
The two orange angles are equal. The two blue angles are equal.

Why are they equal? — The straight-line proof 📐

Here's how to prove it using only what you already know.

Step 1
a° + b° = 180° (angles on a straight line)
Step 2
b° + a° = 180° (the other straight line — same rule)
Conclusion
Both times we get 180°, so the two a° angles must be equal. Same for the two b° angles. Opposite angles are always equal!

Using the rule — finding all four angles 🔢

When two lines cross and you know ONE angle, you can find ALL FOUR.

Two lines cross. One angle is 70°. Find all four angles.
1
Vertically opposite → the angle directly across = 70°
2
Adjacent angle (on straight line) = 180 − 70 = 110°
3
The 4th angle = vertically opposite to 110° = 110°
✅ Four angles: 70°, 110°, 70°, 110°
One angle known → always get: angle, 180−angle, angle, 180−angle

Where exactly do the labels go? ⚠️

A common mistake is putting all the labels near the centre. Each label must go inside its own sector — clearly separated.

❌ Wrong — bunched together

Can't tell which angle is which!

✅ Correct — spread out

Each label is in its own region.

The rule
RIGHT sector = one angle  ·  LEFT sector = vertically opposite (same size)
TOP sector = adjacent angle  ·  BOTTOM sector = vertically opposite (same size)

Think of the crossing as a compass: labels go NORTH, SOUTH, EAST, WEST — not all in one corner.

Vertically opposite + algebra ⭐

ISA sometimes labels the crossing angles with expressions. Since vertically opposite angles are equal, you just set them equal and solve — exactly like SimEq!

Two lines cross. One angle is (3x + 12)° and the vertically opposite angle is (5x − 4)°. Find x and the angle size.
1
Vertically opposite → set equal: 3x + 12 = 5x − 4
2
Collect: 12 + 4 = 5x − 3x → 16 = 2x → x = 8
3
Angle = 3(8) + 12 = 24 + 12 = 36°
4
Check: 5(8) − 4 = 40 − 4 = 36° ✓ Both equal!
✅ x = 8, angle = 36°
Key rule
Vertically opposite → expressions are equal → solve like algebra. Always check by substituting back into both expressions.

Summary — Vertically Opposite Angles 🚀

The rule
Two lines cross → opposite angles are always equal. Adjacent angles add to 180°.
One angle given
Four angles = angle°, (180−angle)°, angle°, (180−angle)°
Algebra version
Set the two opposite expressions equal → solve → check by substituting both.
Why they're equal
Each pair adds to 180° with the same adjacent angle → so both must be equal.
Crossing lines → opposite = equal · adjacent = 180°
