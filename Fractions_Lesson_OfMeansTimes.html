📐 Route B — The OF-Sentence

If the bar made sense, this is how to write it. If it didn't, this might click instead.
① Find the fraction. ② Find what it is OF — the amount it acts on. ③ Write fraction × amount.

Ken drinks 5/9 of the milk in a jug. The jug holds 3/5 L. How much milk does Ken drink?

OF-sentence: 5/9 (the fraction) of 3/5 L (what it's OF)
5/9 × 3/5
cancel 5 (top) with 5 (bottom), 3 (top) with 9 (bottom) → 1/3 × 1/1
= 1/3 L

🗣 The Verb Doesn't Decide — The QUESTION Does

Pours out, uses, eats, spends, cuts off, gives away — these tell you WHICH PART. They do not mean take away.

A bottle has 1 1/2 L of water. Mia pours out 1/6 of the water.

(a) How much did she pour out?

1/6 × 1 1/2 = 1/6 × 3/2
= 1/4 L (× only)

(b) How much is left?

1 1/2 − 1/4
= 1 1/4 L (× first, THEN take away)

🧩 The Sorter — Four Cards, One Word Apart

A fraction WITH a unit (1/4 kg) is an amount → add or take away. A fraction OF something → times. Shared equally → divide.
(i)  Tom had 5/6 kg of flour. He used 1/4 kg. How much is left?
(ii)  Tom had 5/6 kg of flour. He used 1/4 OF it. How much did he use?
(iii)  Tom had 5/6 kg of flour. He used 3/5 of it. How much is left?
(iv)  3/4 L of juice is shared equally among 3 cups. How much is in each cup?

🚩 Too Big? Then It's Wrong

A recipe needs 3 3/5 cups of flour. Dan makes only 5/6 of the recipe.

The right way:

5/6 × 3 3/5 = 5/6 × 18/5
= 3 cups

A wrong way, shown so you can spot it:

3 3/5 ÷ 5/6 = 4 8/25
that's MORE flour than the WHOLE recipe — impossible for part of a recipe
Part of something is always LESS than the whole thing. If your answer grew, you used the wrong operation.
⭐ Your Turn

Practice — "Of" Means Times

P1.  A jar holds 7/8 kg of honey. 2/7 of the honey is used. How much honey is used?
2/7 × 7/8 — cancel 7 with 7 → 2/1 × 1/8
= 1/4 kg
P2.  Ali has 1 1/3 hours of free time. He spends 3/4 of it gaming. How much free time is left?
gaming: 3/4 × 1 1/3 = 3/4 × 4/3 = 1 hour
left: 1 1/3 − 1
= 1/3 hour
P3.  Lin had 9/10 L of paint. She used 1/5 L. How much is left?
1/5 L is an AMOUNT (has a unit) — take away
9/10 − 1/5 = 9/10 − 2/10
= 7/10 L
P4.  A recipe uses 1 7/8 cups of sugar. Ben makes 2/5 of the recipe. How much sugar does he use?
2/5 × 1 7/8 = 2/5 × 15/8 — cancel 2 with 8, 5 with 15 → 1/1 × 3/4
= 3/4 cup (smaller than the recipe — makes sense)
📖 Today's Big Idea

A fraction OF anything means times. A fraction WITH a unit is an amount — add or take away. Part of something is always smaller than the whole thing.
