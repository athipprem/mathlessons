🪙 Grab 40 Coins

Or buttons, or Lego pieces — anything you can count. Try this with the cupcake story:

  1. Make a pile of 24 and a pile of 16.
  2. The question says the baker packs them into boxes of 8. Look at your two piles — do you pack each pile on its own, or push them together first?
  3. Push them together. That push is the bracket. Write it: (24 + 16) ÷ 8
  4. Now try it the other way, WITHOUT pushing: 24 + 16÷8 = 24 + 2 = 26 boxes. Look at your coins — 26 boxes from 40 coins is impossible. That's how you catch it.

Two Worked Examples, Side By Side

✅ 24 cupcakes + 16 cupcakes, packed into boxes of 8

Yes — you pack the WHOLE pile.

(24 + 16) ÷ 8 = 5
❌ 4 packs of stickers at 8 baht, plus a pen for 15 baht

No — the pen never joins the packs.

4 × 8 + 15 = 47
Does an add or subtract have to happen BEFORE a × or ÷? If yes, brackets.

⚠️ Not every add needs brackets. Both stories above contain an addition, and only ONE of them needs brackets — that's the actual thing to check, not "is there an add in here."

Now You Try

1.  Four friends share the cost of a pizza (320 baht) plus a 40-baht delivery fee, equally. How much does each friend pay?
2.  James buys 3 notebooks at 12 baht each, plus a 10-baht pencil. How much does he spend?

You've got it if you can say WHY the first one needs brackets and the second one doesn't — not just get both numbers right.
