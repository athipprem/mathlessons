You asked for this one, so here it is. You already know the rule — top times top, bottom times bottom — you used it on the Unit 58 test and got both right. This lesson is about what the answer MEANS, and a trick that keeps the numbers small.

"Of" means times. Half OF a half is a quarter.

📐 Route B — The Same Thing In Numbers

Same job as the grid, written instead of drawn. The number of green cells is top × top. The number of all cells is bottom × bottom.

4/5 × 3/4

4/5 × 3/4 = (4 × 3)/(5 × 4) top × top = green cells; bottom × bottom = all cells
= 12/20
divide top and bottom by 4
= 3/5

🍕 "Of A Fraction" In A Story, On The Bar

5/6 of a pizza was left. Maya ate 3/5 of what was left. What fraction of the WHOLE pizza did Maya eat?

3/5 × 5/6 = 15/30
= 1/2
the bar shows it too: 3 of the 6 original parts

"Of what was left" = times the fraction that was left.

🐢 Route A For Cancelling — The Long Way First

4/9 × 3/8

4/9 × 3/8 = (4 × 3)/(9 × 8)
= 12/72
both divide by 12
= 1/6

It works, but 72 is a big number to simplify. Next screen: a way to never get the big number.

✂ Route B — Cancel First

Simplifying at the end divides the top and the bottom by the same number. You can do that dividing BEFORE you multiply — it's the same division, just earlier.

4/9 × 3/8

4 (a top) and 8 (a bottom) share 4 → 1/9 × 3/2
3 (a top) and 9 (a bottom) share 3 → 1/3 × 1/2
= 1/6
Cancel a TOP with a BOTTOM. Never a top with a top.

Practice — Fraction of a Fraction

P1.  Work out 7/12 × 4/21.
7 (top) and 21 (bottom) share 7 → 1/12 × 4/3
4 (top) and 12 (bottom) share 4 → 1/3 × 1/3
= 1/9
P2.  4/5 of a garden is grass. Daisies grow on 5/6 of the grass. What fraction of the whole garden has daisies?
5/6 × 4/5 = 20/30
= 2/3
P3.  Work out 9/16 × 4/15.
9 and 15 share 3; 4 and 16 share 4 → 3/4 × 1/5
= 3/20
P4.  A bottle holds 9/10 litre. Rosa drinks 2/3 of it. How much does she drink?
2/3 × 9/10 = 18/30, both divide by 6
= 3/5 litre
📖 Today's Big Idea

Multiplying fractions: top × top, bottom × bottom. You can cancel a top with a bottom BEFORE you multiply, to keep the numbers small. "Of" means times.
