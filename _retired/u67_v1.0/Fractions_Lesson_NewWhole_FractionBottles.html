Fill both lines, like yesterday. Read (a) again before you write (a).

Eva has 2 2/5 L of juice. She drinks 3/8 of the juice. (a) How much juice does she drink? (b) How much juice is left?
Taken in round 1 = 3/8 × 2 2/5"3/8 of the juice" means 3/8 × the whole
= 3/8 × 12/52 2/5 = 12/5 (2 wholes = 10 fifths, plus 2 fifths = 12 fifths)
= 36/40tops: 3 × 12 = 36; bottoms: 8 × 5 = 40
= 9/10 Lsimplify: divide the top and the bottom by 4
Left after round 1 = 2 2/5 − 9/10the whole take away the taken piece
= 12/5 − 9/10change the whole to an improper fraction: 2 2/5 = 12/5 (2 wholes = 10 fifths, plus 2 fifths = 12 fifths)
= 24/10 − 9/10make the bottoms the same: 12/5 = 24/10 (top and bottom × 2)
= 15/1024 tenths take away 9 tenths = 15 tenths
= 3/2simplify: divide the top and the bottom by 5
= 1 1/2 L3 halves = 1 whole and 1 half over

📏 Worked — Two Bars

Kim has 2 2/5 L of juice. She drinks 1/6 of the juice. Then she pours out 3/4 of the juice that was left. How much juice is left?

Watch for the glowing yellow line: that is where the NEW whole is written.

🟦 shaded = taken piece    ⬜ dashed green = left-over piece, which slides down and becomes the NEW whole

Taken in round 1 = 1/6 × 2 2/5"1/6 of the juice" means 1/6 × the whole
= 1/6 × 12/52 2/5 = 12/5 (2 wholes = 10 fifths, plus 2 fifths = 12 fifths)
= 12/30tops: 1 × 12 = 12; bottoms: 6 × 5 = 30
= 2/5 Lsimplify: divide the top and the bottom by 6
Left after round 1 = 2 2/5 − 2/5the whole take away the taken piece
= 12/5 − 2/5change the whole to an improper fraction: 2 2/5 = 12/5 (2 wholes = 10 fifths, plus 2 fifths = 12 fifths)
= 10/512 fifths take away 2 fifths = 10 fifths
= 2 L10 ÷ 5 = 2
New whole = 2 Lround 2: "of the juice that was LEFT" — the left-over piece is the NEW whole
One piece = 2 ÷ 4cut the 2 L bar into 4 equal pieces
= 1/22 ÷ 4 = 1/2, so one piece is 1/2 L
Taken in round 2 = 1/2 × 3the taken piece is 3 of those pieces (the top number)
= 1 1/2 L1/2 × 3 = 1 1/2
Left after round 2 = 2 − 1 1/2the whole take away the taken piece
= 2 − 3/2change the taken piece to an improper fraction: 1 1/2 = 3/2 (1 whole = 2 halves, plus 1 half = 3 halves)
= 4/2 − 3/2make the bottoms the same: 2 wholes = 4 halves
= 1/2 L4 halves take away 3 halves = 1 half
Answer: 1/2 L of juice is left.

💧 Told, Then Of — With A Fraction Bottle

Raj has 3 1/4 L of paint. He uses 3/4 L of the paint. Then he uses 3/5 of the paint that was left. How much paint is left?

Left after round 1 = 3 1/4 − 3/4the whole take away the taken piece
= 13/4 − 3/4change the whole to an improper fraction: 3 1/4 = 13/4 (3 wholes = 12 quarters, plus 1 quarter = 13 quarters)

= 2 1/2 L5 halves = 2 wholes and 1 half over
New whole = 2 1/2 Lround 2: "of the paint that was LEFT" — the left-over piece is the NEW whole
Taken in round 2 = 3/5 × 2 1/2"3/5 of the paint that was left" means 3/5 × the whole
= 3/5 × 5/22 1/2 = 5/2 (2 wholes = 4 halves, plus 1 half = 5 halves)
= 15/10tops: 3 × 5 = 15; bottoms: 5 × 2 = 10
= 3/2simplify: divide the top and the bottom by 5
= 1 1/2 L3 halves = 1 whole and 1 half over
Left after round 2 = 2 1/2 − 1 1/2the whole take away the taken piece
= 5/2 − 1 1/2change the whole to an improper fraction: 2 1/2 = 5/2 (2 wholes = 4 halves, plus 1 half = 5 halves)
= 5/2 − 3/2change the taken piece to an improper fraction: 1 1/2 = 3/2 (1 whole = 2 halves, plus 1 half = 3 halves)

📐 Round 0 — When The Whole Is An Area

A garden is 3 1/3 m long and 2 1/4 m wide. 2/5 of the garden is planted with roses. The rest is grass. What is the area of the grass?

First find the whole: the area. Then take 2/5 of it.

🟦 shaded = taken piece    ⬜ dashed green = left-over piece, which slides down and becomes the NEW whole

Whole = 3 1/3 × 2 1/4round 0: the whole is the area of the garden — length × width
= 10/3 × 2 1/4change the length to an improper fraction: 3 1/3 = 10/3 (3 wholes = 9 thirds, plus 1 third = 10 thirds)
= 10/3 × 9/4change the width to an improper fraction: 2 1/4 = 9/4 (2 wholes = 8 quarters, plus 1 quarter = 9 quarters)
= 90/12tops: 10 × 9 = 90; bottoms: 3 × 4 = 12
= 15/2simplify: divide the top and the bottom by 6
= 7 1/2 m²15 halves = 7 wholes and 1 half over
Taken in round 1 = 2/5 × 7 1/2"2/5 of the garden" means 2/5 × the whole
= 2/5 × 15/27 1/2 = 15/2 (7 wholes = 14 halves, plus 1 half = 15 halves)
= 30/10tops: 2 × 15 = 30; bottoms: 5 × 2 = 10
= 3 m²30 ÷ 10 = 3
Left after round 1 = 7 1/2 − 3the whole take away the taken piece
= 15/2 − 3change the whole to an improper fraction: 7 1/2 = 15/2 (7 wholes = 14 halves, plus 1 half = 15 halves)
= 15/2 − 6/2make the bottoms the same: 3 = 6/2 (top and bottom × 2)
= 9/215 halves take away 6 halves = 9 halves
= 4 1/2 m²9 halves = 4 wholes and 1 half over

Change BOTH sides to improper fractions — one line each. (2 1/2 is 5/2: 2 wholes = 4 halves, plus 1 half.)

Answer: 4 1/2 m² of grass.
