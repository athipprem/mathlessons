## STRUCTURE — the Chapter Block (Lui's, adopted in full)
**Chapter Block = [1–2 Core units synced to class] + [1 Stretch unit beyond class at Int/Applied tiers].**
The Stretch unit is the ISA engine and is never a cold start — it extends what school taught two weeks earlier.
**A chapter now spans 2–3 units instead of being crushed into one** (Lui, 9 Sep). The MAX-2-lessons-per-unit cap
is unchanged; a chapter simply gets 4–6 lessons across a block. Split by *idea*, and give a chapter's abstract
half its own unit — U60 is the worked example of what happens otherwise (L1 concrete 5/5, L2 inversion 0/5, same
unit, same day).

**THE SYNC RULE (load-bearing): never start a Chapter Block more than +1 chapter ahead of Prem's live class.
Confirm his actual chapter with Lui before every Core unit.** If school runs slow, wait — an idle week is far
cheaper than another U60. Cadence 1 unit/week; school runs ~3 weeks/chapter.

**The ten units (revised 11 Sep 2026 — Ch.3 now spans three units; the standalone Geometry Re-Tier is gone):**
U61 Fraction of a set + fraction as division (Core) · U62 Fractions↔decimals + comparing/ordering (Core) ·
U63 Ch.3 word problems at Applied (Stretch) · U64 Area of Triangles Core (strike three on the reverse formula —
build depends on M6 Q9) · U65 Area Stretch · U66 Ratio Core · U67 Ratio Stretch · U68 Volume Core ·
U69 Volume Stretch · U70 Statistics & Data II. **M6 fires first, before U61. M7 after U70.**
Geometry radar is served by U65 and U69 rather than a cold-start re-tier paper.

**How to divide a chapter's topics — by shared mental model, never by count.** The overload risk is the number
of *unrelated new ideas*, not topics. U58 carried four fraction topics and scored 15/15 independently because
they all ran on one machinery; U60 carried ONE topic and collapsed because its second lesson introduced formula
inversion, an unrelated move. **Two topics per unit when they share a model; one per unit when the topic hides a
second unrelated move, especially an inversion.** In Ch.3 only reverse word problems hide one, which is why they
get their own Stretch unit (U63).
**Already banked by U58, independently, 15/15 — do NOT re-teach:** ± mixed numbers, product of proper/improper
fractions, mixed number × whole. They reappear only inside U63's harder word problems.

**The one deliberate sync-rule exception is U70 Statistics** — 5B content run early because it is visual and
concrete (graphs, tables can be pointed at), and ISA can't wait for December. **Decimals and Percentage do NOT
get this exception** — computational and abstract, exactly the U60 profile. They wait for school, cycle 8.

**Deferred, do not reopen in cycle 7:** Art of Counting Total-Minus-Bad (shelved U57), painted-cube edge/face,
formal C(n,r). None is on the February path. **M6 settled this: the deliberate U57 probe at its gentlest form —
the exact shape he answered 4/4 on at U56's third attempt — came back BLANK, as did M6 Q13. Brick 1's content did
not survive. The counting track resets completely whenever it reopens; do not treat any of it as banked.** If they return it is near competition season, taught by Lui with
objects in hand, never again as self-study HTML.

   with the logic in words: "what do I write down"). Prem picks. One extra screen, and it is the only mechanism
   that substitutes for someone answering "but why 6?" in the moment.
   (b) **`{Unit}_SelfCheck_U0XX.html`** — 5 auto-scored questions after both lessons, before the test. 4–5 right
   = "go take the test"; 0–3 = names the concept and sends him to **the other route by screen name**, never back
   to the one that just failed him.
   (c) **`{Unit}_StuckCard_U0XX.html`** — the old Coach Card content rewritten *to Prem* so he uses it alone, on
   the unit's hardest concept: "ask yourself first" (one question he answers before reading on), "go and get
   these" with physical objects named and the demo as instructions he follows, the same demo as a widget for when
   the objects aren't to hand, two contrasted worked examples with a reason on every number, then two questions
   with Check buttons and "you've got it if you can say WHY". **Last line only:** *"Still stuck after all this?
   Tell Dad — that means the lesson has a problem, not you."* Opening a Stuck Card is a win, not a failure —
   track it, don't target it.

6. **TWO-STRIKE RULE + REVISION REVIEW NOTE.** A failed lesson gets ONE revision; if that also fails, stop
   building and write **`{Unit}_ReviewNote_U0XX.md`** into `Memory Backup\`, then tell Lui. One page: what was
   taught (both routes), what Prem actually did (question-level evidence, quote anything he wrote on the paper),
   what the revision changed and the hypothesis behind it, why that hypothesis now looks wrong, and what you

   concrete half, park the abstract half — U60's exact shape). **U64's reverse-formula lesson is strike three on

   **Consequence to state plainly:** with no human channel anywhere, content that cannot be self-taught has no
