Before We Start — Yesterday's Stories

Fill both boxes, like yesterday.

(a)  A rope is 25 m long. Leo cuts off 2/5 of the rope.
The story says 2/5 OF the rope — work out the taken piece first (a piece of the bar)
Taken = 2/5 × 25 = 10 m
Left over = 25 − 10 = 15 m (whole take away the taken piece)
(b)  A bag has 24 kg of flour. Jo uses 5/8 of the flour.
The story says 5/8 OF the flour — work out the taken piece first (a piece of the bar)
Taken = 5/8 × 24 = 15 kg
Left over = 24 − 15 = 9 kg (whole take away the taken piece)
(c)  What is 3/4 of 44 L?

📏 Route A — The Bar

A bottle has 2 4/5 L of juice. Tia drinks 1/4 of the juice.

Cut the bar into 4 equal pieces (the 4 in 1/4). Each piece is 2 4/5 ÷ 4 = 7/10 L. Shade 1 piece.

🟦 shaded = taken piece    ⬜ white = left-over piece

Taken piece = 7/10 L
Left-over piece = 2 1/10 L

🔢 Route B — The Same Bottle, As Numbers

Same as Lesson 1, just with a fraction bottle.
Taken = 1/4 × 2 4/5 = 1/4 × 14/5 = 14/20 = 7/10 L
Left over = 2 4/5 − 7/10 = 28/10 − 7/10 = 21/10 = 2 1/10 L
Never 2 4/5 − 1/4 here! That takes away a quarter of a LITRE, not a quarter of the JUICE.

📝 Only One Question Asked? Fill Both Boxes Anyway

A tank has 3 3/4 L of water. Leo uses 2/5 of the water. How much water is left?

Cut the bar into 5 equal pieces. Each piece is 3 3/4 ÷ 5 = 3/4 L. Shade 2 pieces.

🟦 shaded = taken piece    ⬜ white = left-over piece

Taken = 2/5 × 3 3/4 = 2/5 × 15/4 = 30/20 = 3/2 = 1 1/2 L
Left over = 3 3/4 − 1 1/2 = 2 1/4 L
Taken piece = 1 1/2 L
Left-over piece = 2 1/4 L
The taken box is your stepping stone — always fill it first.

Practice — Fill Both Boxes

Draw the bar on your paper first.

P1.  A ribbon is 2 1/4 m long. Kim cuts off 2/3 of the ribbon. (a) How much ribbon does Kim cut off? (b) How much ribbon is left?
The story says 2/3 OF the ribbon — work out the taken piece first (a piece of the bar)
Taken = 2/3 × 2 1/4 = 2/3 × 9/4 = 3/2 = 1 1/2 m
Left over = 2 1/4 − 1 1/2 = 3/4 m (whole take away the taken piece)
P2.  A jug has 1 1/2 L of milk. Tom pours out 1/4 L of the milk. How much milk is left?
The story TELLS us how much was taken: 1/4 L — so that is the taken piece
Left over = 1 1/2 − 1/4 = 1 1/4 L (whole take away the taken piece)
P3.  A bag has 3 1/3 kg of sand. Raj gives away 1/5 of the sand. How much sand is left?
The story says 1/5 OF the sand — work out the taken piece first (a piece of the bar)
Taken = 1/5 × 3 1/3 = 1/5 × 10/3 = 2/3 kg
Left over = 3 1/3 − 2/3 = 2 2/3 kg (whole take away the taken piece)
P4.  A tub has 1 3/4 kg of butter. Jo uses 3/7 of the butter. How much butter is left?
The story says 3/7 OF the butter — work out the taken piece first (a piece of the bar)
Taken = 3/7 × 1 3/4 = 3/7 × 7/4 = 3/4 kg
Left over = 1 3/4 − 3/4 = 1 kg (whole take away the taken piece)

Finish-the-question check: did you answer the EXACT question asked, is your fraction in simplest form, and did you write the UNIT?
