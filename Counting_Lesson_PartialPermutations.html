The Key Question

Always ask this first: "How many slots am I filling?"

Before you draw a single box, count the slots — the positions that need to be filled. Then draw exactly that many boxes and count down.

Step 1: How many slots to fill? Call it k.
Step 2: How many items available? Call it n.
Step 3: Write k boxes. Fill them: n, n−1, n−2 … stop after k boxes.
Step 4: Multiply.

Watch out — Fixed Positions

When some slots are fixed, they count as 1 choice

This is exactly what caught you in Unit 5 Q5: "4-digit password. First digit must be 1, last must be 9. Middle two digits chosen from {2,3,4,5}, no repeats."

Fixed slots always get a 1. The free slots count down from how many options are left.

Fixed position rule: Write a 1 in every fixed slot. Then fill the free slots by counting down from the available pool.
