⚖️ MATCH & CANCEL move — "two different shopping trips, two totals"

Trigger words: two kinds of things with unknown prices/weights, bought in two different combos, two totals given.
Method: write both lines up. Find the MATCHING part. Subtract → the matching part cancels → one unknown left.

🔄 UNITS SHIFT move — "a ratio that CHANGES after giving / removing / joining"

➡️ Trained in Lesson 3.

Two Receipts, One Trick

A shop sells pens and rulers but lost the price list. We only have two receipts:

🧾 Receipt 1
2 pens + 1 ruler  = ฿11
🧾 Receipt 2
2 pens + 3 rulers = ฿21

Look at the green parts — they MATCH. Both receipts have exactly 2 pens. So any difference in price must come from the red parts!

Subtract the receipts

Receipt 2 − Receipt 1:← big one first
(2 pens + 3 rulers) − (2 pens + 1 ruler) = 21 − 11
2 pens cancel → 2 rulers = ฿10 → 1 ruler = ฿5← the matching part vanishes!
Back into Receipt 1: 2 pens + 5 = 11 → 2 pens = 6 → pen = ฿3← substitute to find the other
✓ Check Receipt 2: 6 + 15 = 21 ✓
🔑 The Match & Cancel Chain

1️⃣ Line up the two facts  2️⃣ Find the match  3️⃣ Subtract (match cancels)  4️⃣ Solve the one left  5️⃣ Substitute back  6️⃣ Check with the other receipt.

What If Nothing Matches? Make a Match!

3 pads + 2 pens = ฿24   and   6 pads + 5 pens = ฿51. No matching part… yet.

DOUBLE the first receipt: 6 pads + 4 pens = ฿48← everything ×2, total too!
Now pads match: (6 pads + 5 pens) − (6 pads + 4 pens) = 51 − 48
1 pen = ฿3
Back in: 3 pads + 6 = 24 → pad = ฿6
✓ Check: 6×6 + 5×3 = 36 + 15 = 51 ✓

⚠ When you multiply a receipt, multiply EVERYTHING — every item AND the total. Forgetting the total is the classic slip.

Second worked example (same-first-item type)

4 cookies + 2 milks = ฿26.  1 cookie + 2 milks = ฿14.

Milks match (2 = 2). Subtract: 3 cookies = 12 → cookie = ฿4
1×4 + 2 milks = 14 → 2 milks = 10 → milk = ฿5
✓ Check: 16 + 10 = 26 ✓

Practice

Line up → match → subtract → solve → substitute → check.

Q1.   5 buns and 2 teas cost ฿31. 3 buns and 2 teas cost ฿21. Find the price of one bun and one tea.
Teas match. Subtract: 2 buns = 10 → bun = ฿5
3×5 + 2 teas = 21 → 2 teas = 6 → tea = ฿3
✓ Check: 25 + 6 = 31 ✓
Q2.   A cap and 3 pairs of socks cost ฿19. The same cap and 5 pairs of socks cost ฿27. Find the price of one pair of socks and the cap.
Caps match. Subtract: 2 socks = 8 → socks = ฿4
cap + 12 = 19 → cap = ฿7
✓ Check: 7 + 20 = 27 ✓
Q3.   A toy with 4 batteries costs ฿22. The same toy with 9 batteries costs ฿37. Find the price of one battery and the toy.
Toys match. Subtract: 5 batteries = 15 → battery = ฿3
toy + 12 = 22 → toy = ฿10
✓ Check: 10 + 27 = 37 ✓
Q4.   2 kiwis and 3 plums cost ฿18. 4 kiwis and 5 plums cost ฿34. Find the price of one kiwi and one plum. (Nothing matches — make a match first!)
Double receipt 1: 4 kiwis + 6 plums = ฿36← total doubles too!
Subtract receipt 2: (4k+6p) − (4k+5p) = 36 − 34 → plum = ฿2
2 kiwis + 6 = 18 → kiwi = ฿6
✓ Check: 24 + 10 = 34 ✓
📖 What You Learned Today
The trigger:Two unknown prices + two different combos + two totals = MATCH & CANCEL. The chain:Line up → match → subtract → solve → substitute → check. No match?Multiply one receipt (EVERYTHING, including the total) until something matches. Always check:Put both prices back into the OTHER receipt.

  • You remember the total when doubling a receipt
  • You always substitute back to find the second unknown
