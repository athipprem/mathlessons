The red ribbon, both ways

The ratio of the length of a red ribbon to the length of a blue ribbon is 3 : 7. The blue ribbon is 20 m long. Find the length of the red ribbon, correct to 2 decimal places.
✂️ Multiply first
Top = 20 × 3DIVIDE LAST: multiply first. WANT: the red ribbon is 3 boxes, and 20 is 7 boxes
= 6020 × 3 = 60
60 ÷ 7 ≈ 8.57 mnow divide, just once: 60 ÷ 7 = 8.571… The next digit is 1, so keep it
Divide first
20 ÷ 7 ≈ 2.86Rounded already. This is the tiny error.
2.86 × 3 = 8.58 ✗The tiny error got multiplied by 3.
8.58 is wrong. The answer is 8.57.Dividing first makes a tiny error. Multiplying makes it bigger.
Multiply first. Divide last. Round only once.
