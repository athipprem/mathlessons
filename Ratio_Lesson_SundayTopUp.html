📌 THE 3 HABITS
★1 ASKED FOR WHAT? Before you write the answer, point at the question: one part? the OTHER part? ALTOGETHER? how many MORE? Then use THAT many units.
★2 BACK-CHECK every simplest form: × your answer back up. You must get the numbers in the question (7 × 8 = 56, but the question says 54 ✗).
★3 ROUND ONCE: keep 1 unit as a fraction (50 ÷ 7 = 7 1/7). Multiply. Only the LAST line gets rounded.
