👋 Brand New — Let's Start Simple

Why learn this?

Sometimes we want to know "how many different ways can I make this?" — how many outfits, how many meals, how many lock codes. The answers get big fast, so we want a quick way instead of drawing them all.

There are only two counting moves you ever need. We'll learn just ONE today, slowly, until it's easy. (The other one is the next lesson.)

Today's ONE idea:
when you BUILD or ARRANGE something → MULTIPLY

See It First — 2 Hats and 3 Scarves

You have 2 hats (red, blue) and 3 scarves (green, yellow, pink). How many different hat-and-scarf sets can you wear?

Let's just list them all:

red + greenred + yellowred + pink blue + greenblue + yellowblue + pink

That's 6 sets. Now look: each hat went with 3 scarves → 3 + 3 = 6. That is the same as 2 × 3.

🔑 The idea

When you pick one from each group to build something, you multiply the sizes of the groups. No drawing needed.

More Groups, and "In a Row"

More than two groups? Keep multiplying.

2 shirts, 3 shorts, 2 hats → outfits =

2 × 3 × 2 ← one slot per group
= 6 × 2 = 12 outfits

Putting things in a ROW (this is still building → multiply)

How many ways to line up 4 different books on a shelf? Fill the first spot, then the next, counting down what's left:

1st spot: 4 choices
2nd spot: 3 left
3rd spot: 2 left
4th spot: 1 left
4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 24 ways

"1st and 2nd place" is the same kind — just two spots

1st and 2nd medal from 5 runners:

1st: 5 choices, 2nd: 4 left
5 × 4 = 20 ← just multiply, nothing else

💡 Building, outfits, lining up, 1st & 2nd — all the SAME move: multiply. Don't add. Don't divide.

Your Turn

Every one of these is "multiply". Take your time.

Q1.   A shop has 5 sandwiches and 2 drinks. How many different sandwich-and-drink lunches are there?
build → multiply: 5 × 2 = 10
Q2.   In how many ways can 4 different toy cars be lined up in a row?
in a row → 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 24
Q3.   Prem has 2 caps, 3 t-shirts and 2 pairs of shoes. How many cap-shirt-shoes outfits can he make?
2 × 3 × 2 = 12
Q4.   From 5 children, in how many ways can a 1st-place and a 2nd-place prize be given?
two spots → 5 × 4 = 20
📖 Move 1 — in one line

If you are BUILDING or ARRANGING → MULTIPLY the choices.

Outfits, meals, codes, lining up, 1st & 2nd — all multiply. Nothing is added or divided.

🏆 You've got it when...

  • You see "outfits / meals / line up / 1st & 2nd" and just multiply
  • For a row, you count down: 4 × 3 × 2 × 1
