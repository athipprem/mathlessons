The Pair Method

Start from 1. Work up to √N. List pairs.

Every factor has a partner. If 2 × 12 = 24, then both 2 and 12 are factors of 24. Finding one automatically gives you the other.

The 3-step system:
1. Start with 1 × N   (always works)
2. Try 2, 3, 4, 5 … — keep the ones that divide evenly
3. Stop when you reach √N — you've found all pairs

Let's see it with 24.   √24 ≈ 4.9, so we stop after testing 4.
