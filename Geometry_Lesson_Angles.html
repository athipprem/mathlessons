Triangle — angles always add to 180° ▲

No matter what shape a triangle is — big, small, tilted — its three angles always sum to exactly 180°.

Any triangle: angle₁ + angle₂ + angle₃ = 180°
So: missing angle = 180° − (sum of the other two)
Find the missing angle x in a triangle where the other two are 65° and 72°.
1
x + 65 + 72 = 180
2
x + 137 = 180
3
x = 180 − 137 = 43°
✅ x = 43°

💡 Isosceles shortcut — used in ISA!

If a triangle has two equal sides, the two base angles are equal. So if you know one base angle is 50°, the other is also 50°, and the apex = 180 − 50 − 50 = 80°.

Three more rules — straight line, crossing lines, and a point 🔵

Rule 1 — Straight line = 180°

Rule 2 — Vertically Opposite Angles are EQUAL

When two straight lines cross, the angles opposite each other are always equal.

Rule 3 — Angles at a point = 360°

All angles around a single point add up to 360°. Think of a full circle.

Straight line: a + b = 180°  |  Full turn: all angles = 360°
Vertically opposite: the two facing angles are always equal

Quadrilateral — angles add to 360° ◻

Any four-sided shape (square, rectangle, trapezium, rhombus, irregular) has angles summing to 360°.
Why? Split it into two triangles — each triangle has 180°, so 2 × 180° = 360°.

A quadrilateral has angles 95°, 110°, 85°, and x°. Find x.
1
95 + 110 + 85 + x = 360
2
290 + x = 360
3
x = 70°
✅ x = 70°

Finding unknowns algebraically ⭐ — your strength!

ISA loves combining angle rules with algebra. This is where your algebra skills give you a huge advantage.

🔑 The method — always the same

Write the equation using the angle sum rule.
Collect like terms. Solve for x.
Check by substituting back.

Example 1: A triangle has angles x°, 2x°, and 30°. Find x.
1
x + 2x + 30 = 180  (triangle rule)
2
3x + 30 = 180 → 3x = 150
3
x = 50°  → angles are 50°, 100°, 30°
✅ x = 50° · Check: 50 + 100 + 30 = 180 ✓
Example 2: Two angles on a straight line are (3x + 10)° and (2x + 20)°. Find x.
1
(3x + 10) + (2x + 20) = 180  (straight line)
2
5x + 30 = 180 → 5x = 150
3
x = 30°  → angles are 100° and 80°
✅ x = 30° · Check: 100 + 80 = 180 ✓
Example 3: Vertically opposite angles. One is (4x − 10)° and the opposite is (2x + 30)°. Find x.
1
Vertically opposite → they are equal: 4x − 10 = 2x + 30
2
2x = 40 → x = 20°
✅ x = 20° · Angle = 4(20)−10 = 70°
