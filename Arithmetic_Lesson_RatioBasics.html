What is a Ratio?

A ratio compares two quantities. 3 : 2 means "for every 3 of the first thing, there are 2 of the second."

Ratio 3:2
Key vocabulary:
• The ratio 3:2 has 5 total parts (3+2)
• The ratio 2:5 has 7 total parts (2+5)
• Each "part" has the same value — we find it by dividing the total or difference

Two types of ratio problems appear in ISA:

A
Given the total — find each amount
e.g. "Ratio 2:3, total = 20, find each part"
B
Given the difference — find each amount
e.g. "Ratio 2:5, difference = 21, find each part" ← this was U8 Q14
Type A

Ratio + Total → Find Each Part

📘 Worked Example

Question: Red and blue marbles are in the ratio 2 : 3. There are 20 marbles in total. How many of each colour?

Step 1: Count total parts: 2 + 3 = 5 parts
Step 2: Value of 1 part: 20 ÷ 5 = 4
Step 3: Multiply: Red = 2 × 4 = 8  |  Blue = 3 × 4 = 12
Check: 8 + 12 = 20 ✓
Red = 8  |  Blue = 12
Formula (Total type):
1 part = Total ÷ (sum of ratio)
Each amount = ratio number × (1 part)
Practice

Try It

Q1. Boys and girls in a class are in ratio 3 : 4. There are 28 students. How many boys?

3+4=7 parts  |  28÷7=4  |  Boys = 3×4 = 12

Q2. Money is shared in ratio 5 : 3. Total = $48. Find the larger share.

5+3=8 parts  |  48÷8=6  |  Larger = 5×6 = $30
Type B — ISA Favourite

Ratio + Difference → Find Each Part

This is the type from U8 Q14 — the one left blank. It looks harder but uses the same idea.

📘 Worked Example

Question: Two amounts are in ratio 2 : 5. The difference between them is 21. Find each amount.

Step 1: Difference in parts: 5 − 2 = 3 parts
Step 2: Value of 1 part: 21 ÷ 3 = 7
Step 3: Multiply: Smaller = 2 × 7 = 14  |  Larger = 5 × 7 = 35
Check: 35 − 14 = 21 ✓  |  14 : 35 = 2 : 5 ✓
Smaller = 14  |  Larger = 35
Formula (Difference type):
1 part = Difference ÷ (difference of ratio numbers)
Each amount = ratio number × (1 part)
Practice

Try It

Q3. Two bags of rice are in ratio 3 : 7. The heavier bag has 16 kg more. How heavy is the heavier bag?

7−3=4 parts  |  16÷4=4  |  Heavier = 7×4 = 28 kg

Q4. Leo saved $15 more than Maya. Their savings are in ratio 8 : 3. How much did Leo save?

8−3=5 parts  |  15÷5=3  |  Leo = 8×3 = $24

Ratio — Two Methods, One Key Step

Every ratio problem starts the same way: find the value of 1 part. Then multiply.

Given TOTAL: 1 part = Total ÷ (a + b)
Given DIFFERENCE: 1 part = Difference ÷ (b − a)

Then: Amount A = a × (1 part)  |  Amount B = b × (1 part)

Always check your answer: add both amounts → should equal the total (if total type), or subtract → should equal the difference (if difference type).
