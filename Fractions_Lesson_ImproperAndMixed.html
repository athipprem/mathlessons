Write 3 2/5 as an improper fraction.

🧩 Two-Part Questions — Where You Lost Q10

A 17-metre rope is cut into 5 equal pieces. (a) How long is each piece? (b) Three pieces are joined. How long is the joined rope?

(a) 17 ÷ 5 = 3 remainder 2
(a) 3 2/5 m
(b) 3 × 3 2/5 → 3 × 3 = 9, and 3 × 2/5 = 6/5 = 1 1/5
(b) 9 + 1 1/5 = 10 1/5 m

Part (b) always uses part (a). If (a) is blank, (b) can't happen — so write SOMETHING for (a), even a guess.

Practice — Improper & Mixed

P1.  Write 7/4 as a mixed number.
7 ÷ 4 = 1 remainder 3
= 1 3/4
P2.  Write 2 1/3 as an improper fraction.
2 × 3 = 6.   6 + 1 = 7
= 7/3
P3.  30 metres of tape is cut into 7 equal pieces. How long is each piece?
30 ÷ 7 = 4 remainder 2
= 4 2/7 m
P4.  4 cakes are shared equally among 3 children. How much does each child get?
4 ÷ 3 = 1 remainder 1
= 1 1/3 cakes
📖 Today's Big Idea

A division that doesn't come out even: the remainder becomes the top of the fraction, and the number you divided by stays on the bottom. To go the other way, multiply the whole number by the bottom, add the top.
