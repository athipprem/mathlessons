Unit 21 · Lesson 2

A new challenge — n on BOTH sides! 🤔

In Lesson 1, all the n's were on one side. Easy! But what happens when n appears on both sides?

3n + 12 = 5n − 4

We have 3n on the left AND 5n on the right. We can't solve it like this. We need to collect all the n's onto one side and all the numbers onto the other side.

⚠️ The sign flip rule
When a term crosses the = sign, its sign flips: + becomes − and − becomes +.
Think of it as: you're subtracting it from both sides to move it across.

The Two-Move Method 🎯

Every equation with n on both sides is solved with the same two moves:

Move 1: Collect all n's to the LEFT
Subtract the smaller n group from both sides
Move 2: Collect all numbers to the RIGHT
Add or subtract the number to move it across the = sign
Example — Solve: 3n + 12 = 5n − 4
Move 1 — Get all n's to the LEFT
Subtract 3n from both sides (it's the smaller group):
3n + 12 − 3n = 5n − 4 − 3n
→  12 = 2n − 4
Move 2 — Get all numbers to the RIGHT
Add 4 to both sides:
12 + 4 = 2n − 4 + 4
→  16 = 2n
Finish — Divide by 2
16 ÷ 2 = 2n ÷ 2  →  n = 8
✓ n = 8  |  Check: 3×8+12 = 36  =  5×8−4 = 36 ✓

More examples 📖

Example 2 — Solve: 7n − 3 = 4n + 9
Move 1 — n's to the LEFT (subtract 4n)
7n − 3 − 4n = 4n + 9 − 4n  →  3n − 3 = 9
Move 2 — numbers to the RIGHT (add 3)
3n = 9 + 3  →  3n = 12
Finish — divide by 3
n = 4
✓ n = 4  |  Check: 7×4−3 = 25  =  4×4+9 = 25 ✓
Example 3 — Solve: 6n + 5 = 2n + 25
Move 1 — subtract 2n from both sides
4n + 5 = 25
Move 2 — subtract 5 from both sides
4n = 20
Finish — divide by 4
n = 5
✓ n = 5  |  Check: 6×5+5 = 35  =  2×5+25 = 35 ✓
💡 Quick shortcut tip
You can write it in one clean line:
6n + 5 = 2n + 25  →  6n − 2n = 25 − 5  →  4n = 20  →  n = 5
n's move left (subtracted), numbers move right (subtracted). Signs flip when crossing =.

Lesson 2 Complete! 🏆

You've mastered equations with variables on both sides. Here's the complete method:

The Full Equation Method
Step 1: Expand any brackets first
Step 2: Move all n's to the LEFT (subtract the smaller n group from both sides)
Step 3: Move all numbers to the RIGHT (add/subtract to move them across =)
Step 4: Divide both sides to find n
Step 5: Check your answer by substituting n back in
✅ Correct approach
3n + 12 = 5n − 4
→ 12 + 4 = 5n − 3n
→ 16 = 2n
→ n = 8
❌ Common mistake
Forgetting to flip the sign when crossing the = sign.
−4 becomes +4 when it moves to the left side.
