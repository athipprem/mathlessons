The New Rule

See the trigger word. Draw the tree. Immediately.

There are only 3 trigger words you need to recognise. When you see any of them, your hand moves to the paper before your brain finishes reading the question.

🔎 Watch for these words:

factorise   prime factors   express as a product of primes

Any of these = draw your tree first, read the rest second.

Practice 2 of 3

Draw your tree on paper first. Then fill in the answer below.

Hint: Try 72 ÷ 2 = 36. Now factorise 36 (you already know that one!).
So 72 = 2 × 36 = 2 × 2² × 3² = 2³ × 3²
72 = 2 × 3
72 = 2³ × 3² ✓
Practice 3 of 3 — same as the test!

Factorise 180 into prime factors.

Draw your full tree on paper. 180 has three different prime factors.

Starting point: 180 ÷ 2 = 90. Then 90 ÷ 2 = 45. Then 45 ÷ 3 = 15. Then 15 ÷ 3 = 5. Done.
180 = 2 × 3 × 5
180 = 2² × 3² × 5 ✓

The prime 5 appears once so its power is 1 — no need to write the ¹.
