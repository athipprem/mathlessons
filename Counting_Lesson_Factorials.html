Lesson 1 — Factorial

When ALL items are arranged: use factorial

When you fill ALL positions with ALL items (no repeats), every box counts down by 1. This countdown has a shortcut called factorial, written with an exclamation mark !

4! = 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 24
5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 120
3! = 3 × 2 × 1 = 6

Rule: n! = n × (n−1) × (n−2) × … × 2 × 1

Start from n and count down to 1. Multiply them all together.

Lesson 2 — Partial Permutations

What if you don't fill ALL positions?

Sometimes you only pick a few items from a larger group. You still count down — but you stop when your boxes are full.

Example: 5 runners race. How many ways can they finish 1st, 2nd, 3rd?

You have 3 positions to fill, from 5 runners. Count down, stop after 3 boxes:

5
1st place

4
2nd place
×

=

Key rule: Boxes = positions to fill. Start from total available, count down, stop when boxes run out.

Lesson 3 — Fixed Positions

When one position is locked, give it 1 choice

If a specific item must go in a specific position, that box gets 1 (no choice to make). Then count down normally for the rest.
