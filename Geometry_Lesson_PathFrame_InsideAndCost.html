🗺️ Your Frame Pocket Map

You already nailed the outside frame questions last time — outer path around a pool, frame around a photo, all correct. The two that slipped were the INSIDE border and the COST one. This lesson trains exactly those two moves (marked TODAY). Two questions decide every frame problem:

🔍 1) Is the border OUTSIDE or INSIDE?    2) Do they want AREA or COST?

➕ OUTSIDE — "path around / frame around" (you own this)

Given shape is the SMALL one. Outer = given + 2×width each way. Already solid — quick warm-up only.

➖ INSIDE — "border / margin left inside, carpet in the middle"

Given shape is the BIG one. Inner = given − 2×width each way. This is the U26 Q4 slip — trained today.

💰 COST — "costs ฿___ per square metre"

Two boxes: find the AREA first, then area × price. Never multiply by price too early. The U26 Q5 slip — trained today.

Move 1 — INSIDE means SHRINK

When the border is inside, the shape they GIVE you is the big outer one. The thing you want (carpet, rug, mat) is the small inner one. So you subtract the border from each side.

➕ OUTSIDE (you already do this)

OUTER = shape + path
GIVEN
(small)

Outer = given + 2×width

➖ INSIDE (today)

GIVEN = the room (big)
CARPET
(find this)

Inner = given − 2×width

🔑 Always 2× the width

The border hugs both edges — left AND right, top AND bottom. A 3 m border takes 6 m off each dimension, never 3. This is true going out (+6) or in (−6).

❌ U26 Q4: room 36×30, border 3 m inside, carpet = ? The answer is (36−6)×(30−6) = 30×24 = 720. The slip was forgetting to shrink. Draw the nested box — the carpet is clearly the small inner one.

Move 2 — COST is TWO boxes

When a question says "costs ฿___ per square metre", it has two parts. Do them in order and never mix them up:

BOX 1
Find the AREA
(big − small)
→
BOX 2
AREA × price
= money

Area first, money last. If you multiply by the price before you have the right area, every later number is wrong.

Worked Example A  Garden 25×15 m, path 2 m around OUTSIDE, ฿120 per m². Cost?

BOX 1: outer = (25+4)×(15+4) = 29×19 = 551← +4 (2 m both sides)
       garden = 25×15 = 375
       path area = 551 − 375 = 176 m²
BOX 2: cost = 176 × 120 = ฿21,120← area FIRST, money LAST

Worked Example B  Floor 44×26 m. A 2 m border INSIDE is polished at ฿90 per m². Cost of the border?

Inner rug = (44−4)×(26−4) = 40×22 = 880← inside = shrink
BOX 1: border = 44×26 − 880 = 1144 − 880 = 264 m²← they want the BORDER, not the rug
BOX 2: cost = 264 × 90 = ฿23,760

Read carefully: do they want the middle piece, or the border ring? The border ring = big − small.

Practice

Draw the nested box first. Outside (+) or inside (−)? Area or cost? Then answer.

Q1.   A room is 28 m × 20 m. A carpet leaves a bare border 2 m wide all around inside. What is the area of the carpet?
Inside → SHRINK: carpet = (28−4)×(20−4)
= 24 × 16 = 384 m²
Q2.   A swimming pool is 15 m × 9 m. A path 2 m wide is built all around the outside. The path is tiled at ฿180 per square metre. What does the path cost?
BOX 1: outer = (15+4)×(9+4) = 19×13 = 247
pool = 135. path = 247 − 135 = 112 m²
BOX 2: 112 × 180 = ฿20,160
Q3.   A square hall floor is 40 m × 25 m. Tiles cover the middle, leaving a bare strip 5 m wide all around inside. What is the area of the tiled part?
Inside → SHRINK: tiled = (40−10)×(25−10)← 5 m both sides = −10
= 30 × 15 = 450 m²
Q4.   A garden is 26 m × 18 m. A concrete path 2 m wide is laid around the outside. Concrete costs ฿250 per square metre. What is the total cost of the path?
BOX 1: outer = (26+4)×(18+4) = 30×22 = 660
garden = 468. path = 660 − 468 = 192 m²
BOX 2: 192 × 250 = ฿48,000
📖 What You Locked In Today
Draw first:Two nested rectangles, before any numbers. Always. Outside:Given is SMALL → outer = given + 2×width. Inside:Given is BIG → inner = given − 2×width. Border ring:big area − small area (not the middle piece). Cost = 2 boxes:AREA first, then area × price. Money last.

🏆 You are ready when...

  • You say out loud "inside = shrink" or "outside = grow" before calculating
  • You never multiply by the price until the area is finished
  • You re-read to check: do they want the middle piece, or the border ring?
