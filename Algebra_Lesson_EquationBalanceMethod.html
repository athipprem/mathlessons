What is an equation? ⚖️

An equation is like a balance scale. Both sides must weigh exactly the same. The equals sign = is the pivot in the middle.

3n + 4 = 19

Our job is to find the value of n that makes both sides equal. To do that, we must keep the scale balanced — whatever we do to one side, we MUST do to the other side too.

🥇 The Golden Rule of Equations

Whatever you do to one side — do it to the other side too!
Add the same number to both sides. Subtract the same number from both sides. The scale stays balanced.
⚠️ Break this rule and the scale tips!
If you subtract 4 from the left but not the right, they're no longer equal. Always treat both sides exactly the same.

Solving simple equations 🔢

When all the n's are on one side, solving is straightforward. We just undo what's been done to n.

Example 1 — Solve: 4n = 20
→n is multiplied by 4. To undo it, divide both sides by 4.
→4n ÷ 4 = 20 ÷ 4
✓ n = 5
Example 2 — Solve: 2n + 3 = 13

Two things have been done to n: multiplied by 2, then added 3. Undo them in reverse order.

Step 1 — Remove the number (+3)
Subtract 3 from both sides:  2n + 3 − 3 = 13 − 3  →  2n = 10
Step 2 — Remove the multiplier (×2)
Divide both sides by 2:  2n ÷ 2 = 10 ÷ 2  →  n = 5
✓ n = 5  |  Check: 2×5 + 3 = 13 ✓
Example 3 — Solve: 3n − 1 = 17
Step 1 — Remove the −1
Add 1 to both sides:  3n − 1 + 1 = 17 + 1  →  3n = 18
Step 2 — Remove the ×3
Divide both sides by 3:  3n ÷ 3 = 18 ÷ 3  →  n = 6
✓ n = 6  |  Check: 3×6 − 1 = 17 ✓

Equations with brackets 🔣

Sometimes an equation has brackets. Always expand the brackets first, then solve as normal.

Example — Solve: 5(n + 2) = 30
Step 1 — Expand brackets
5 × n + 5 × 2 = 30  →  5n + 10 = 30
Step 2 — Remove the +10
5n = 30 − 10  →  5n = 20
Step 3 — Divide by 5
n = 4
✓ n = 4  |  Check: 5×(4+2) = 5×6 = 30 ✓
The two-step method
Step 1: Move the number to the right side (add or subtract)
Step 2: Divide both sides to find n
+ number → subtract it
3n + 4 = 19
→ 3n = 19 − 4
→ 3n = 15 → n = 5
− number → add it
3n − 6 = 9
→ 3n = 9 + 6
→ 3n = 15 → n = 5
