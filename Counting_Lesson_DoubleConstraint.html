Q10 was blank. Here's why — and how to fix it.

The good news: it's the exact same Fix & Remove method — just applied twice instead of once.

🌟 Double Constraint Unlocked!

Fix & Remove works for any number of constraints.

The method — always the same:
1. Draw all boxes (one per position)
2. Write 1 in every fixed slot
3. Remove ALL fixed items from pool
4. Count down for free slots from pool size
5. Multiply everything together

2 fixed positions, 3 fixed positions — same steps. Just remove more items from the pool.
