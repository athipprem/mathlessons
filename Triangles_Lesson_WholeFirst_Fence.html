📌 CHAPTER 4 CARD
▲1 Pick a BASE. ▲2 Its HEIGHT meets the base line at a square corner ⊾ — slanted sides are decoys; the height can be outside.
▲3/▲4 Area of a triangle = base × height ÷ 2 (half its box). Square units: cm², m².
▲7 Shaded part = whole − the white parts you can find.
▲7+ WHOLE FIRST. Whole = the full outline (made of 2 or 3 squares? add them up first). White = the parts that are NOT shaded. Shaded = Whole − White. Never add a white part to the shaded answer.
▲8 Base on one side of a rectangle, tip on the opposite side = HALF the rectangle, wherever the tip is.
▲9 Odd shape? SPLIT it into rectangles and triangles. ADD the pieces — or WHOLE − the piece taken away.
▲9+ COUNT THE PIECES. Tap each piece once and write × the count. Pieces along an edge? Count = edge ÷ one base.
▲10 Perimeter given? Find the missing side first: perimeter − the sides you know.
▲10+ WALK THE FENCE. The perimeter goes round the OUTSIDE once, corner to corner. A line inside the shape (like BE) is NOT on the fence. A side made of two parts: add them (DA = AE + ED).
▲11 Same base + same height = same area. A line from a corner to the MIDDLE of the opposite side cuts a triangle into two equal halves.
▲12 Why ÷ 2? Two copies of the same triangle make a rectangle (box) — one triangle is half of it.
