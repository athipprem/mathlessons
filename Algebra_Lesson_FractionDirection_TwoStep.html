Fraction Direction — Two-Step

Unit 10  ·  Algebraic Thinking  ·  ~15 mins

Review

One-step fraction problems — you've got this.

In Unit 9 you got Q11 correct. Let's make sure the method is clear before adding a second step.

Q11 type: "Gave away 2/5 of his sweets. Has 18 left. How many did he start with?"

Gave away 2/5 → kept 3/5
3/5 of total = 18
Total = 18 ÷ (3/5) = 18 × 5/3 = 30

The key rule: always work with the kept fraction, not the given-away fraction. Then divide the leftover amount by the kept fraction.

Formula: Total = leftover ÷ (kept fraction)

Fraction Direction Two-Step — Complete!

You extended the one-step method into two steps. The pattern is always the same:

1. Find the kept fraction at each step.
2. Multiply all the kept fractions together.
3. Divide the final leftover by that product.

If a question has three operations, same method — just multiply three kept fractions before dividing.
