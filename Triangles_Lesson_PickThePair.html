🪝 Two Steps: The Pair You Know, Then The Pair You Want

Same triangle rule, used twice. Each line says which card line it uses.

the pair you KNOWgivesthe area× 2 ÷ the side you knowthe side you WANT

Worked example:

Route B — two steps. Triangle A has a base of 10 cm and an area of 45 cm². Triangle B has the SAME height and a base of 14 cm. Find B's area.

Area = 45 cm²triangle A: area 45 cm², base 10 cm
Box = 45 × 2▲5 undo the half: the box (base × height) is twice the triangle
= 9045 × 2 = 90
Height of A = 90 ÷ 10▲6 the box is base × height, so the missing side = box ÷ the base of A (10)
= 9 cm90 ÷ 10 = 9
10 × 9 = 90✔3 back-check: base of A × my answer gives the box again
90 ÷ 2 = 45✔3 and half the box is the area in the question ✓
Base = 14 cm▲1 triangle B: base 14
Height = 9 cm▲2 the SAME height as A
Rectangle = 14 × 9▲3 base × height = the box the triangle fits in
= 12614 × 9 = 126
Area of B = 126 ÷ 2▲4 the triangle is half of the box
= 63 cm²126 ÷ 2 = 63; area is in square units

Two steps again. A right-angled triangle has sides 15 cm, 20 cm and 25 cm. Find the height from the square corner to the 25 cm side.

Base = 20 cm▲1 use the pair you KNOW: the 20 cm side
Height = 15 cm▲2 its height is the 15 cm side (they meet at the square corner)
Rectangle = 20 × 15▲3 base × height = the box the triangle fits in
= 30020 × 15 = 300

= 150 cm²300 ÷ 2 = 150; area is in square units
Area = 150 cm²same triangle, same area — now the 25 cm side is the base

= 300150 × 2 = 300

📌 MY CARDS
THE TRIANGLE CARD
▲1 Pick a BASE — any side can be the base.
▲2 Its HEIGHT = the straight-up line from the opposite corner down to the base line. It meets the base line at a square corner ⊾. A slanted side is NOT the height. If the top corner hangs past the end of the base, stretch the base line — the height is outside.
PICK THE PAIR: the base, and the line that meets it at a square corner ⊾. Slanted sides are decoys. A right-angled triangle has 3 pairs.
▲3 Rectangle = base × height (the box the triangle fits in).
▲4 Area = rectangle ÷ 2 (the triangle is half the box). Write square units: cm², m².
▲5 Missing height or base? UNDO THE HALF first: box = area × 2.
▲6 The box is base × height, so the missing side = box ÷ the side you know.
ANSWER CHECK — on every question

✔2 Copy check: is every number in my working the same as in the question?
✔3 Back-check: undo my last step (× ↔ ÷). Do I get the number I started with? (27 × 2 = 54 ✓ · 28 × 2 = 56 ✗)
FREE ITEMS
G “For every 3 you buy, 1 is free”: one GROUP = bought + free (3 + 1 = 4). Full groups = people ÷ group size. The extra people pay. Paid = groups × bought + extras.
THE BIG PICTURE
① Taken part = fraction × whole “1/4 OF the juice”
Told? “3/4 L” → Taken part = 3/4 L. No × needed!
1/3 OF 12 bricks: the bricks split into 3 equal groups, one group lights up, giving 4.
1/3 × 12 = 12/3 = 4. ‘Of’ means ×.
1/3 kg is just a small lump — that’s TOLD, not ×.
② Left part = whole − taken part
③ “… of what was LEFT”? → the left part is the NEW whole. Back to ①.
④ LAST CHECK: read the question again — does it ask for a TAKEN part, the LEFT part, or HOW MUCH MORE?
“How much more?” = bigger part − smaller part (that’s the − side too)
×0 Mixed number? Make it improper.
2 1/4 = 9/4   Whole number? 12 = 12/1
×1 top × top, bottom × bottom
×2 Simplify → mixed number
Example:
5/6 × 1 4/5
= 5/6 × 9/5 ×0
= 45/30 ×1
= 3/2 ×2
= 1 1/2 ×2
−0 Mixed number? Make it improper.
−1 Make the bottoms the same.
Count up in the BIGGER bottom until the smaller one fits:
thirds & quarters → 4, 8, 12 ✓ → twelfths
−2 top − top (the bottom stays!)
−3 Simplify → mixed number
Example:
3 1/2 − 3/4
= 7/2 − 3/4 −0
= 14/4 − 3/4 −1
= 11/4 −2
= 2 3/4 −3
+0 Mixed? Make it improper.
+1 Same bottoms (count up in the bigger bottom).
+2 top + top (bottom stays!)
+3 Simplify → mixed number
÷ FRACTIONS ARE DIVISIONS
a ÷ b = a/b   5 ÷ 6 → 5/6 each   13 ÷ 4 = 13/4 = 3 1/4
Improper → mixed: top ÷ bottom = wholes, remainder = the new top.
. FRACTION → DECIMAL
Make the bottom 10, 100 or 1000:   3/4 = 75/100 = 0.75
Can’t? Divide top by bottom:   1/3 = 0.333…
≈ ROUND to 2 decimal places: look at the 3rd digit → 5 or more, round up. 0.333… ≈ 0.33
B WORKING BACKWARDS (“how much at FIRST?”)
1. Left fraction = 1 − taken fraction
2. That many units = the amount you know
3. 1 unit = amount ÷ units
4. Whole = 1 unit × the bottom number
Two rounds? Undo the LAST round first, then the first round.
