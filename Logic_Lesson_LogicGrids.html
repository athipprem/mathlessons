🕵️ Be a Detective

Why learn this?

A good detective doesn't guess — they use clues to cross out what can't be true until only one answer is left. That's exactly how you crack a code, solve an escape-room puzzle, or work out "who owns which pet" from a few hints. Contests love these — and the trick is always the same: make a table and eliminate. No more "I don't know" — you'll always have a first move.

Use each clue to CROSS OUT what's impossible.
When only one box is left in a row, that's the answer.

The Tool: a Yes/No Grid

Put the people down the side and the things across the top. Each clue lets you write ✗ (can't be) or ✓ (yes, it is) in a box.

Golden move: once a box is ✓, the WHOLE row and WHOLE column get ✗ (each person has just one pet, each pet has just one owner).

🗺️ The Detective's Plan

1Draw the grid: people down the side, things across the top.
2Take each clue and mark ✗ for "can't be" and ✓ for "is".
3After each ✓, cross out the rest of that row AND column.
4When a row has only ONE empty box left, that box is a ✓.

Tip: start with the clue that tells you the MOST (an "is" clue, or a person with two crosses).

Worked Example

Ann, Ben and Cara each have one pet: a cat, a dog or a fish.
Clue 1: Ann has neither the cat nor the dog.   Clue 2: Ben has the dog.

Clue 1 — Ann: cross out cat and dog → only fish is left → Ann = fish ✓
Now cross out "fish" for Ben and Cara (Ann took it).
Clue 2 — Ben = dog ✓. Cross out "dog" for Cara.
Cara has only "cat" left → Cara = cat ✓

Ann = fish, Ben = dog, Cara = cat. Solved — no guessing!

Your Turn

Cross out what can't be true, then see what's left. Make a little grid if it helps.

Q1.  Mia, Leo and Zoe each pick a different fruit: apple, banana or cherry. Mia does NOT pick apple or banana. Which fruit does Mia pick?
Cross out apple and banana for Mia → only cherry left.
Mia = cherry
Q2.  In a race, Lee finished ahead of Kim, and Kim finished ahead of Sam. Who finished LAST?
Order: Lee → Kim → Sam.
Sam is last.
Q3.  Anna, Bob and Cy wear a red, a blue and a green hat. Anna's hat is NOT red and NOT green. Bob's hat is green. What colour is Cy's hat?
Anna: not red, not green → Anna = blue.
Bob = green. So Cy gets what's left = red.
Cy = red
Q4.  Pam, Rex and Sid each play one instrument: piano, guitar or drums. Pam plays drums. Rex does NOT play guitar. What does Sid play?
Pam = drums. Rex ≠ guitar and ≠ drums → Rex = piano.
Only guitar is left for Sid.
Sid = guitar
📖 Logic Grids — in one line

Make a table, use each clue to cross out what's impossible, and a ✓ crosses out its whole row and column.

When only one box is left in a row, that's your answer. You never have to guess.

🏆 You've got it when…

  • You turn "Ann isn't the cat or dog" into two crosses → fish is left
  • After a ✓, you cross out the rest of that row and column automatically
