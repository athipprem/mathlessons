🔍 Tell If It Divides — Without Dividing

A number is divisible by another if it splits evenly with no remainder. Instead of doing the whole division, there are quick rules that tell you the answer in seconds. They all look at either the last digit(s) or the digit sum.

🔍 Which divisor are you testing?

👁️ LOOK AT THE END — for 2, 5, 10 (and 4)

2 → last digit even. 5 → last digit 0 or 5. 10 → last digit 0. 4 → last TWO digits make a number that ÷ 4.

➕ ADD THE DIGITS — for 3 and 9

3 → digit sum ÷ 3. 9 → digit sum ÷ 9.

🔗 COMBINE TWO RULES — for 6

6 = 2 × 3, so a number ÷ 6 only if it passes BOTH the ÷2 and the ÷3 checks.

Look at the End — 2, 5, 10

For these three, you only need the very last digit. Ignore everything else.

2Last digit is even (0, 2, 4, 6, 8).   3,476 → ends in 6 → yes
5Last digit is 0 or 5.   8,205 → ends in 5 → yes
10Last digit is 0.   5,940 → ends in 0 → yes

And 4 — look at the last TWO digits

A number is ÷ 4 if the number made by its last two digits is ÷ 4.

Add the Digits — 3 and 9

Add up all the digits. If that digit sum is divisible by 3, so is the whole number. Same idea for 9.

Example: is 5,124 divisible by 3?

Example: is 6,453 divisible by 9?

6 + 4 + 5 + 3 = 18 → 18 ÷ 9 = 2 → YES

💡 If the digit sum is still big, add its digits again. 18 → 1+8 = 9. Anything whose digits eventually reach 9 is divisible by 9 (and 3).

Combine Two Rules — 6

6 = 2 × 3. So a number is divisible by 6 only if it passes both checks: even AND digit sum ÷ 3. Miss either one, and the answer is no.

Example: is 5,418 divisible by 6?

÷2? ends in 8 → even ✓
÷3? 5+4+1+8 = 18 → ÷3 ✓
both pass → YES, divisible by 6

❌ Careful: 5,128 is even, but 5+1+2+8 = 16, and 16 is not ÷ 3. So 5,128 is not divisible by 6. You must pass BOTH.

Same trick for 15: 15 = 3 × 5, so check ÷3 (digit sum) AND ÷5 (ends in 0 or 5).

📖 The Rules on One Card
÷ 2:last digit even (0,2,4,6,8). ÷ 5:last digit 0 or 5. ÷ 10:last digit 0. ÷ 4:last TWO digits make a number ÷ 4. ÷ 3:digit sum ÷ 3. ÷ 9:digit sum ÷ 9. ÷ 6:passes ÷2 AND ÷3.
