◆1 ORDER — the ratio follows the order you name

Two ways to see it. First you line them up. Then the same thing in lines.

👥 Part and whole. A team has 9 boys and 6 girls. Girls : the whole team?

Whole team = 15. Girls : whole team = 6 : 15. Both can be ÷ by 3 → 2 : 5.
Same thing in lines: name the order, write a : b, then simplest form.
L1
A tray has 9 cupcakes and 4 muffins. (a) What is the ratio of the number of muffins to the number of cupcakes? (b) What is the ratio of the number of cupcakes to the number of muffins?
Muffins : cupcakes = 4 : 9◆1 the question says MUFFINS first, then cupcakes
Cupcakes : muffins = 9 : 4◆1 now CUPCAKES first — the order flips
L2
A team has 9 boys and 6 girls. Find the ratio of the number of girls to the number of children in the whole team. Give your answer in its simplest form.
Team = 9 + 6◆2 the whole team = boys + girls
= 159 + 6 = 15
Girls : team = 6 : 15◆2 girls first, then the WHOLE team (not the boys)
= 2 : 5◆3 ÷ both by 3 — the biggest number that goes into 6 and 15

📌 RATIO CARD
◆1A ratio compares two amounts: a : b. ORDER matters — write the amounts in the order the question names them.
◆2Part : total? The total is all the parts added together.
◆3Simplest form: ÷ BOTH numbers by the biggest number that goes into both (just like simplifying a fraction).
◆4Equivalent ratios: × or ÷ BOTH numbers by the SAME number. Missing number? Find that number first.
◆5Work it out first: 'more than', 'in 3 years' time', 'after giving' — find the real amounts FIRST, then write the ratio.
◆6A ratio has NO unit. Length → cm or m · area → cm² or m² · ratio → no unit.
