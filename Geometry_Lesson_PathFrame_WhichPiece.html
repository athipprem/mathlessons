🗺️ One Last Thing to Lock In

Your last test proved the hard part is done: you shrink for inside borders correctly, and you got the trickiest question (inside border + cost) perfect. Two small habits cost the marks. Fix these and Path & Frame is yours for good.

🔍 1) Which PIECE do they want?    2) If cost: area FIRST, then × the price

🟩 The MIDDLE piece — "carpet, rug, mat, tiles, lawn"

This is the inner rectangle. Take it directly: (length − 2×width) × (width − 2×width). No subtracting from the room.

🟧 The RING piece — "border, path, frame, strip"

This is the big area − small area. (Works for inside OR outside borders.)

💰 COST — "costs ฿___ per square metre"

Find the correct AREA first, then area × the price in the question. Check the price digits before you multiply.

The Same Picture, Two Different Answers

Every frame question has the same two rectangles. The trap is grabbing the wrong one. Underline the piece-word in the question first.

🟧 RING = border / path
🟩 MIDDLE
carpet / rug

Example: Room 38 m × 24 m, border 3 m wide inside.

MIDDLE (carpet) = (38−6)×(24−6) = 32×18 = 576 m²← take it directly
RING (border) = 38×24 − 576 = 912 − 576 = 336 m²← big − small

Same diagram — two answers. "Find the carpet" → 576. "Find the border" → 336. Read which word they used.

❌ Your last test, Q3: the question asked for the carpet (the middle). You worked out the carpet perfectly (34×22 = 748) — then subtracted it from the room and wrote the border (372). The maths was right; the piece was wrong. Underline the word: carpet = middle = take it directly.

Two checks before you write the answer: (1) did I use the right piece for the area? (2) am I multiplying by the exact price printed in the question?

Worked Example  Garden 30×18 m, path 2 m around OUTSIDE, ฿90 per m². Cost?

BOX 1: outer = (30+4)×(18+4) = 34×22 = 748
       garden = 540. path (RING) = 748 − 540 = 208 m²
BOX 2: 208 × 90 = ฿18,720← price = 90, not anything else

Practice

Underline the piece-word FIRST. Middle = inner rectangle directly. Ring = big − small. Cost = area then × the printed price.

Q1.   A room is 34 m × 24 m. A carpet covers the middle, leaving a border 2 m wide all around inside. What is the area of the carpet?
Carpet = MIDDLE = (34−4)×(24−4) = 30×20 = 600 m²← take it directly
Q2.   A photo is 16 cm × 12 cm. A frame 2 cm wide goes around the outside. What is the area of the frame?
Frame = RING. Outer = (16+4)×(12+4) = 20×16 = 320
Photo = 192. Frame = 320 − 192 = 128 cm²
Q3.   A pond is 22 m × 14 m. A path 2 m wide runs around the outside, tiled at ฿120 per m². What does the path cost?
Outer = (22+4)×(14+4) = 26×18 = 468. Pond = 308.
Path = 160 m². Cost = 160 × 120 = ฿19,200← price = 120
Q4.   A hall is 36 m × 24 m. A rug covers the middle, leaving a border 3 m wide inside. The border is polished at ฿100 per m². What does the polishing cost?
Rug (middle) = (36−6)×(24−6) = 30×18 = 540
Border (RING) = 36×24 − 540 = 864 − 540 = 324← they want the border
Cost = 324 × 100 = ฿32,400
📖 The Whole Concept, In Five Lines
Draw + underline:Nested rectangles, then underline the piece-word. Inside:shrink — inner = given − 2×width. MIDDLE piece:carpet/rug/mat/lawn → the inner rectangle, taken directly. RING piece:border/path/frame → big area − small area. Cost:correct area × the exact price in the question.
