◆10 — “correct to 1 decimal place”: round ONCE, at the very end

A rope is cut in the ratio 2 : 3 : 6. The longest piece is 40 cm. The rope is 11 units long.

L5
A rope is cut into three pieces in the ratio 2 : 3 : 6. The longest piece is 40 cm. Find the length of the rope, correct to 1 decimal place.
1 unit = 40 ÷ 6◆8 the LONGEST piece is 6 units
= 6 2/340 ÷ 6 = 6 2/3
Units = 2 + 3◆7 all the units together
= 52 + 3 = 5
Units = 5 + 6and the next amount's units
= 115 + 6 = 11
Rope = 11 × 6 2/3all 11 units
= 73 1/311 × 6 2/3 = 73 1/3
73 1/3 ≈ 73.3 cm◆10 round once, at the end: the next digit is 3, so keep

⚠️ What goes wrong if you round early?

1 unit = 40 ÷ 6 = 6 2/3 ≈ 6.7 (rounded too early)
11 × 6.7 = 73.7 ✗   The right answer is 73.3.
Keep 6 2/3 exact. Round only the last line.

📌 RATIO CARD
◆1 A ratio compares amounts: a : b. ORDER matters — write them in the order the QUESTION names them, even if you worked the other one out first.
◆2 Part : total? The total is ALL the parts added together.
◆3 Simplest form: ÷ ALL the numbers by the biggest number that goes into all of them.
◆4 Equivalent ratios: × or ÷ ALL the numbers by the SAME number. Missing number? Find that number first.
◆5 Work it out first: 'more than', 'years ago', 'in … years' time', 'after giving' — find the real amounts FIRST (start from NOW), then write the ratio.
◆6 A ratio has NO unit. Length → cm or m · area → cm² or m² · ratio → no unit.
◆7 UNITS: 3 : 5 means 3 equal units and 5 equal units. Find 1 UNIT first — then you can find anything.
◆8 What do you know? The TOTAL → ÷ all the units. ONE part → ÷ its units. The DIFFERENCE → ÷ the units between them.
◆9 Three amounts a : b : c work the same way: order, simplest, units.
◆10 'Correct to 1 (or 2) decimal places': keep the exact answer until the very last line, then round once.
