← Hub 💡 How Do I Know Whether To Times, Take Away Or Divide? — Stuck Card

Before you read anything below — ask yourself:

"Is the fraction OF something — or is it an amount with a unit, like 1/3 L?"

Say your answer out loud. Then scroll down and check.

✂ Go And Get A Strip Of Paper

One strip of paper and a pen.

  1. Write "1 1/2 L of water" along the strip.
  2. Fold it into 3 equal parts and tear one part off. That's "pour out 1/3 OF the water" — it is 1/2 L, and 1 L is left.
  3. Now take a fresh strip, labelled 1 1/2 L, and write "pour out 1/3 L". Nothing to fold — you just take 1/3 L away, and 1 1/6 L is left.
Same numbers, different word, different answer.

No paper handy? Try it here instead:

Both strips start at 1 1/2 L. Same numbers, two different stories.

Two Contrasted Examples, Side By Side

✅ "1/3 OF the water" — times
1/3 × 1 1/2 = 1/3 × 3/2
(fraction OF an amount)
= 1/2 L  ✓
🔶 "1/3 L" — take away
1 1/2 − 1/3 = 9/6 − 2/6
(a fraction WITH a unit — an amount)
= 1 1/6 L  ✓
A fraction OF something → times. A fraction WITH a unit → it's an amount — add or take away.

Now You Try

1.  A bag has 6/7 kg of nuts. Ann eats 1/3 OF the nuts. How much is left?
2.  A bag has 6/7 kg of nuts. Ann eats 1/3 kg. How much is left?

You've got it if you can say WHICH WORD made the answers different.
