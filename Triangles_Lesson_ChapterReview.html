🎨 Shaded Parts and the Half Trick

▲7 — Shaded part = whole − the white parts

Find the WHOLE, find each white part you can, then lift the white parts out.

Shaded parts (Activity 4). A rectangle is 17 cm by 10 cm. Two white corner triangles are cut off: one with sides 6 cm and 10 cm, one with sides 6 cm and 6 cm, each at a square corner. Find the shaded area.

Whole = 17 × 10the whole rectangle: length × width
= 17017 × 10 = 170
White 1 box = 6 × 10▲3 6 cm along the bottom; 10 cm up the side: base × height
= 606 × 10 = 60
White 1 = 60 ÷ 2▲4 a triangle is half its box
= 3060 ÷ 2 = 30
White 2 box = 6 × 6▲3 6 cm along the top; 6 cm down the side: base × height
= 366 × 6 = 36
White 2 = 36 ÷ 2▲4 a triangle is half its box
= 1836 ÷ 2 = 18
Shaded = 170 − 30▲7 shaded = whole − the white parts you can find
= 140170 − 30 = 140
Shaded = 140 − 18▲7 take away the next white part
= 122 cm²140 − 18 = 122

Composite figures (Activity 5). An arrow is a rectangle 10 cm by 4 cm with a triangle on its end. The triangle's base is 8 cm (upright) and its tip is 5 cm from that base. Find the area of the arrow.

📐 Perimeter First, Equal Halves, Why ÷2

▲10 — Perimeter given? Find the missing side first

Perimeter first (Activity 6). Figure ABCD is triangle ABE joined to rectangle BCDE. AE = 12 m, ED = 8 m, CD = 9 m. The perimeter of ABCD is 52 m. (a) Find AB. (b) Find the area of ABCD.

Known sides = 8 + 9BC = ED = 8 m and CD = 9 m
= 178 + 9 = 17
Known sides = 17 + 20and DA = AE + ED = 12 + 8 = 20 m
= 3717 + 20 = 37
AB = 52 − 37▲10 perimeter − the sides you know
= 15 m52 − 37 = 15
Triangle ABE box = 12 × 9▲3 AE = 12 m; BE = 9 m (the rectangle side, square corner at E): base × height
= 10812 × 9 = 108
Triangle ABE = 108 ÷ 2▲4 a triangle is half its box
= 54108 ÷ 2 = 54
Rectangle BCDE = 8 × 9the whole rectangle: length × width
= 728 × 9 = 72
Total = 54 + 72▲9 add the pieces
= 126 m²54 + 72 = 126

▲9 — Identical pieces: work out one, then multiply

A logo is a square of side 6 cm with 4 identical triangles, one on each side. Each triangle's tip is 4 cm out from the square. Find the area of the logo.

Square = 6 × 6the whole rectangle: length × width
= 366 × 6 = 36
One triangle box = 6 × 4▲3 its base is a side of the square, 6 cm; its tip is 4 cm out: base × height
= 246 × 4 = 24
One triangle = 24 ÷ 2▲4 a triangle is half its box
= 1224 ÷ 2 = 12
Four triangles = 12 × 4the 4 triangles are identical
= 4812 × 4 = 48
Total = 36 + 48▲9 add the pieces
= 84 cm²36 + 48 = 84

▲11 — The middle line cuts a triangle into two equal halves

Let's Find Out. In triangle ABC, D is the middle of AC (AD = DC). Triangle BDC has an area of 15 cm². Find the area of triangle ABC.

ABD = 15▲11 AD = DC (same base) and both share the height from B: same area as BDC
ABC = 15 + 15the two halves together
= 30 cm²15 + 15 = 30

▲12 — Why ÷ 2?

Two copies of the same triangle fit together into a rectangle — the box. So one triangle is half of the box.
