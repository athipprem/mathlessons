The Rule

Fix & Remove — 4 steps, every time

1
Draw one box per position — as many boxes as there are slots in total (e.g. 5 boxes for a 5-digit number).
2
Fill fixed positions first — write "1" in each fixed box and label what digit is fixed there. Colour or mark these boxes.
3
Remove the fixed digits from the pool — cross them out from your available digits. Count what's left.
4
Fill the free boxes — count down from the reduced pool size. Multiply everything.
The key question for Step 3: "After fixing those positions, how many digits are still available for the free slots?"

Always answer this before writing any number in a free box.
