🎯 Why — Most Questions Ask For What Is LEFT

Good news:

Most questions don't ask how much was used — they ask how much is LEFT. Good news: you already have the pieces.

Used pieces come off. What is left is a count of pieces too.

❌ Route A — Cross Out The Used Pieces

A tank has 2 2/3 L of water. Leo uses 1/4 of it. How much is left?

Tap the button under the picture, one step at a time.

🔢 Route B — The Numbers

Crossing out pieces is the same as taking away. Keep counting in the same pieces until the very end.
8 thirds − 2 thirds = 6 thirds
6 thirds = 6/3 = 2 L

The same thing, one more way:

what is left is 3/4 of it
3/4 × 2 2/3 = 3/4 × 8/3 = 2 L
⭐ Your Turn

Practice — Fill In The Frame Every Time

P1.  A cake weighs 1 3/5 kg. 3/8 of the cake is eaten. How much cake is left?
The WHOLE is:
Cut it into pieces: pieces of
Working:do this on your paper
Answer (with unit):
WHOLE: 1 3/5 kg
8 pieces of 1/5 kg
8 ÷ 8 = 1, 1 × 3 = 3 pieces eaten
8 − 3 = 5 pieces left
5 pieces of 1/5 kg = 5/5 = 1 kg
P2.  A rope is 4 1/6 m long. 3/5 of the rope is used. How much rope is left?
The WHOLE is:
Cut it into pieces: pieces of
Working:do this on your paper
Answer (with unit):
WHOLE: 4 1/6 m
25 pieces of 1/6 m
25 ÷ 5 = 5, 5 × 3 = 15 pieces used
25 − 15 = 10 pieces left
10 pieces of 1/6 m = 10/6 = 1 2/3 m
P3.  Ana ran 2 1/2 km. Ben ran 4/5 as far as Ana. How far did Ben run?
The WHOLE is:
Cut it into pieces: pieces of
Working:do this on your paper
Answer (with unit):
WHOLE: Ana's run, 2 1/2 km
5 pieces of 1/2 km
5 ÷ 5 = 1, 1 × 4 = 4 pieces
4 pieces of 1/2 km = 4/2 = 2 km
P4.  A rug is 2 1/4 m long and 1 1/3 m wide. 2/3 of the rug is red. What is the red area?
The WHOLE is:
Cut it into pieces: pieces of
Working:do this on your paper
Answer (with unit):
Area of the rug: 2 1/4 × 1 1/3 = 9/4 × 4/3 = 3 m²
WHOLE: 3 m² — that is 3 pieces of 1 m²
3 ÷ 3 = 1, 1 × 2 = 2 pieces
2 pieces of 1 m² = 2 m²
📖 Today's Big Idea

"Left" means: find the used pieces, cross them out, count what remains — in the same pieces, until the very end.

Finish-the-question check: did you answer the EXACT question asked, is your fraction in simplest form, and did you write the UNIT?
