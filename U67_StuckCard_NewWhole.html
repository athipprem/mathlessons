❓ Which whole do I use for the second fraction?

Before you read anything below — ask yourself:

Does the story say ‘of what was LEFT’? Then which bar is the whole now?

Say your answer out loud. Then scroll down and check.

🧱 Go And Get 12 Lego Bricks

12 bricks, all the same size.

  1. “Use 1/4 of them”: take 3 away, and 9 are left.
  2. “Then use 2/3 of what was LEFT”: make 3 piles from the 9, not the 12, and take 2 piles. That’s 6 bricks.
  3. Now try it the wrong way: 2/3 of 12 = 8, but only 9 are left. See? It’s the same words, but the second whole is smaller.

Now You Try

🗺 Open my map

1.  Pia has 2 1/4 L of milk. She drinks 5/9 of the milk. Then she drinks 1/2 of the milk that was left. How much milk is left?
Taken in round 1 = 5/9 × 2 1/4"5/9 of the milk" means 5/9 × the whole
= 5/9 × 9/42 1/4 = 9/4 (2 wholes = 8 quarters, plus 1 quarter = 9 quarters)
= 45/36tops: 5 × 9 = 45; bottoms: 9 × 4 = 36
= 5/4simplify: divide the top and the bottom by 9
= 1 1/4 L5 quarters = 1 whole and 1 quarter over
Left after round 1 = 2 1/4 − 1 1/4the whole take away the taken piece
= 9/4 − 1 1/4change the whole to an improper fraction: 2 1/4 = 9/4 (2 wholes = 8 quarters, plus 1 quarter = 9 quarters)
= 9/4 − 5/4change the taken piece to an improper fraction: 1 1/4 = 5/4 (1 whole = 4 quarters, plus 1 quarter = 5 quarters)
= 4/49 quarters take away 5 quarters = 4 quarters
= 1 L4 ÷ 4 = 1
New whole = 1 Lround 2: "of the milk that was LEFT" — the left-over piece is the NEW whole
One piece = 1 ÷ 2cut the 1 L bar into 2 equal pieces
= 1/21 ÷ 2 = 1/2, so one piece is 1/2 L
Taken in round 2 = 1/2 × 1the taken piece is 1 of those pieces (the top number)
= 1/2 L1/2 × 1 = 1/2
Left after round 2 = 1 − 1/2the whole take away the taken piece
= 2/2 − 1/2make the bottoms the same: 1 whole = 2 halves
= 1/2 L2 halves take away 1 half = 1 half
2.  Max has 2 L of water. He uses 1/2 L of the water. Then he uses 2/3 of the water that was left. How much water is left?
Taken in round 1 = 1/2 Lthe story TOLD us the taken piece: a number with a unit right after it
Left after round 1 = 2 − 1/2the whole take away the taken piece
= 4/2 − 1/2make the bottoms the same: 2 wholes = 4 halves
= 3/24 halves take away 1 half = 3 halves
= 1 1/2 L3 halves = 1 whole and 1 half over
New whole = 1 1/2 Lround 2: "of the water that was LEFT" — the left-over piece is the NEW whole
Taken in round 2 = 2/3 × 1 1/2"2/3 of the water that was left" means 2/3 × the whole
= 2/3 × 3/21 1/2 = 3/2 (1 whole = 2 halves, plus 1 half = 3 halves)
= 6/6tops: 2 × 3 = 6; bottoms: 3 × 2 = 6
= 1 L6 ÷ 6 = 1
Left after round 2 = 1 1/2 − 1the whole take away the taken piece
= 3/2 − 1change the whole to an improper fraction: 1 1/2 = 3/2 (1 whole = 2 halves, plus 1 half = 3 halves)
= 3/2 − 2/2make the bottoms the same: 1 = 2/2 (top and bottom × 2)
= 1/2 L3 halves take away 2 halves = 1 half

You’ve got it if you can say which bar the second fraction cut.
