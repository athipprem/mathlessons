The trick to remember
Just subtract the term numbers: jumps = big number − small number. That's it. T₃ to T₆ = 6−3 = 3. Never add 1, never do anything else.

Decreasing sequences & "which term is zero/negative?"

When d is negative, the sequence goes down. ISA and competition questions love asking: "which term is the first to be zero or negative?"

Q: The sequence is 40, 35, 30, 25, … Which term is the first negative term?
1
a=40, d=−5
2
Tₙ = 40 + (n−1)×(−5) = 40 − 5n + 5 = 45 − 5n
3
First negative: 45 − 5n < 0 → 5n > 45 → n > 9 → first is n = 10
4
Check: T₉ = 45 − 45 = 0 (not negative), T₁₀ = 45 − 50 = −5 ✓
T₁₀ is the first negative term
Shortcut
For decreasing sequences, find when the formula = 0 first. If that gives a whole number, the next term is the first negative. If it gives a decimal, round up to the next integer.
Q: Sequence 100, 93, 86, 79, … Which term is the first below 30?
1
a=100, d=−7. Tₙ = 100 + (n−1)×(−7) = 107 − 7n
2
107 − 7n < 30 → 7n > 77 → n > 11
T₁₂ is the first term below 30. Check: T₁₂=107−84=23 ✓, T₁₁=107−77=30 (not below)
